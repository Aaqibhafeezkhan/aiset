# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want aiset to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel run by the project) and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub: [@JamshidS](https://github.com/JamshidS). Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on the severity, they may respond with:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a period without interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards individuals or groups.
